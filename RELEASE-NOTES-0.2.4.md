# Antria 0.2.4

Signing back in just got easier.

**What's new**

- **Finish sign-in with a code.** Sometimes Claude's sign-in hands you a short code instead of sending you straight back to Antria. Now you can simply paste that code into the sign-in screen to finish — on both Mac and Windows. No more getting stuck after logging in.

Everything you and your team have built — roles, skills, and hires — carries over untouched.
