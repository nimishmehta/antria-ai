# Antria 0.2.3

A smoother setup and a cleaner, more trustworthy workspace.

**What's new**

- **One-tap connections.** Setting up a tool like Vercel is now a single "Install & Sign In." Antria installs it quietly in the background and opens sign-in for you — no terminal, no commands, nothing technical.
- **Cleaner deliverables.** When your team's reply is just a note or a quick confirmation, it stays clean — you get exactly the files that task produced, and never stray files from earlier work.
- **Sharper images, your choice.** Prefer OpenAI's image quality? Switch image generation to ChatGPT in Settings, paste your key once, and your team uses it just for images — Claude stays the default if you'd rather not.

Everything you and your team have built — roles, skills, and hires — carries over untouched.
