# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

This project is a small, friendly place. Everyone is welcome to report a bug, ask a question, or suggest an idea, regardless of experience, background, or how they found their way here.

## What's expected

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Critique the work, not the person.
- Accept that a maintainer may say no, and that "no" is not hostility.
- Keep discussion on topic and in public where possible, so others can benefit.

## What's not acceptable

- Personal attacks, harassment, or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or delete any contribution that violates this
document, and may block repeat offenders from the repository.

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it privately to
**nmehta@bookr.inc**. Reports are handled confidentially and you will get a
response within a week.

This document is adapted from the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), simplified for a
project of this size.
