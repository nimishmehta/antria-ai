## What it is

Antria is a desktop app that turns one sentence into finished work. You point it at a folder, describe what you need in plain English, and a team of AI specialists gets to work — talking it through with each other, pushing back on weak ideas, and landing real files on your desk. No terminal, no prompt engineering, no second cloud bill. It runs on the Claude Code account you already pay for, and your work stays on your machine.

## Watch them think, not just deliver

The work isn't the file at the end — it's the room getting there. A strategist, a writer, an engineer, and a closer work the problem together, out loud, before anything reaches you.

> **Task:** *"Enterprise prospect went quiet after our pricing call. Win them back."*
> GTM says *"offer 15% off."* The Founder rejects it — *"discount before diagnosis; we don't know what broke yet."* Sales re-angles. The team pauses to ask you one question. A follow-up email lands in your drafts — **saved, not sent** — waiting for your go.

That's not a bug in the process. That's the process.

## FAQ

**Isn't "a team" just one model wearing different hats?** No — the specialists collaborate on the same problem at the same time, each bringing the angle only their skill sees. On top of that a Founder debates, rejects, and refines in real time. What comes back has been pressure-tested, not produced in one pass.

**Does it hallucinate?** Every AI can get things wrong. The Founder role exists to catch it — it checks the work against your files and your goal and rejects what drifts.

**Will it burn through my usage?** It's built lean on both ends. A built-in vector search pulls only the context a task needs, and the output is deliberately non-fluff. Less in, less out.

**Do I need another subscription?** No signup, no second cloud subscription. It runs on the Claude or Codex account you already have.

**Do I need to be technical?** No terminal, no code. Point it at a folder and describe what you need in plain language.

**Will it send things without me?** No — anything outward-facing (sending, publishing, deploying, paying) stops and asks you first.

**What if the work isn't right?** Hand it back with a note. The team reworks it in place.
