# SOW & Delivery Kit

Own a signed customer from contract to go-live. This is the services-delivery playbook — the Delivery / Implementation Lead's core toolset. It generates **business templates and plans**, not legal advice (route contract redlines to counsel).

## 1 · Statement of Work (SOW)
A good SOW removes ambiguity before work starts. Sections:
- **Objectives & outcomes** — what "done" means, in the customer's words.
- **Scope** — what's included, and an explicit **out-of-scope** list (this prevents scope creep).
- **Deliverables** — concrete artifacts with acceptance criteria.
- **Timeline & milestones** — phases with dates and dependencies.
- **Responsibilities** — a RACI: what the customer must provide (data, access, sign-offs) vs. what you deliver.
- **Assumptions, fees, and change-control** — how out-of-scope requests get priced and approved.
Deliver as a clean document (use /document-generate or /make-pdf). Flag anything that needs legal review — don't invent contract law.

## 2 · Onboarding runbook
A repeatable checklist to get a new client live: welcome + kickoff scheduling, access/credential collection, environment setup, data migration, configuration, training, and the success criteria for "onboarded."

## 3 · Implementation plan
Phase the work (Discovery → Configure → Migrate → Test → Train → Go-live). Each phase: owner, dates, entry/exit criteria, risks. Track it in a real file (/xlsx for a milestone tracker, or a shared plan).

## 4 · Go-live checklist
Pre-launch verification, rollback plan, day-one support coverage, and a handoff to Customer Success for ongoing retention.

## Quality bar
Every engagement has a written scope with an explicit out-of-scope list, a dated milestone plan, and a named customer responsibility list. Margin and timeline are tracked, not assumed. Handoff to Customer Success is explicit so nothing drops after go-live.

## When NOT to use
Selling/closing the deal → Growth Lead. Ongoing retention after go-live → Support/Customer Success. Contract legal redlines → outside counsel. This kit is the delivery of a signed services engagement.
