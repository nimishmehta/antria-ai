# Email Drafts

Write emails in the founder's voice. This skill drafts only. It is an outward-facing action, so the hard rule below is absolute.

## THE HARD RULE (read this first, out loud)
**Never send an email. Ever. Without the founder saying "send it" in plain words.**
- Draft → show the founder → wait for explicit "send"/"yes send"/"go" → only then send.
- "Looks good", "nice", silence, or a thumbs-up is NOT a send instruction. Ask.
- Creating a draft in Gmail/Outlook is fine and encouraged (it sits unsent). Pressing send is gated.
- When you hand back a draft, state loudly: "This is a draft. Nothing was sent. Say 'send it' when you're ready."

## How to draft
1. Read the thread/context you're replying to (pull it via the Gmail/Outlook connector if the founder points at a real message).
2. Match the founder's voice — how they open, sign off, how formal, sentence length. If you have prior sent mail, mirror it. If not, default to warm, direct, first-person, no corporate filler.
3. Write it short. One screen. One clear ask or next step per email.
4. Always include a subject line.
5. Save it as a draft where the founder works:
   - Gmail connected → create a draft (do not send).
   - Outlook connected → create a draft (do not send).
   - Neither → return the draft as plain text they can paste.
6. Hand back with the send reminder above.

## Concrete example
Founder: "Reply to the VC who asked for our deck — warm, tell them it's coming Friday."
Draft:
> Subject: Deck — sending Friday
>
> Hi Dana,
>
> Great meeting you. I'm tightening the deck and will have it to you Friday. Anything specific you want me to make sure it covers?
>
> Best,
> [Founder]

Saved as a Gmail draft. Nothing sent. Say "send it" when ready.

## Quality bar (every time)
- **Sounds like the founder**, not like an assistant. No "I hope this email finds you well."
- **One ask.** If there are two, it's two emails or a numbered short list.
- **Subject line** always present and specific.
- **Short** — most business email should fit on a phone screen.
- The draft is saved unsent, and the no-send reminder is stated.

## When NOT to use
- Sending, scheduling send, or bulk outreach sequences → that's a send action; get explicit OK, and for cold-outreach campaigns route to Growth Lead.
- A full document (proposal, SOW, letter) → /docx or /document-generate, not an email body.
- Internal comms strategy / brand voice definition → Copywriter + brand-guidelines.
- Meeting agendas or minutes → /meeting-kit.
