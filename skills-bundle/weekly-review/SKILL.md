# Weekly Review

The heartbeat that keeps a solo-ish founder pointed at the right thing. Ruthless prioritization is the whole point — a list of 20 is a failure.

## Monday — triage
1. Gather open work: active tasks in the office, calendar for the week, anything the founder flags.
2. Sort by what actually moves the business, not what's loudest or easiest.
3. Output:
   - **The ONE thing** — if only one thing happens this week, it's this. Name it, justify it in a line.
   - **Also this week** — 3–5 max, ranked.
   - **Not this week** — explicitly parked, so the founder can say no with a clear conscience.
4. Cut hard. If the list is long, you haven't prioritized yet.

## Friday — recap
1. Against Monday's plan:
   - **Done** — shipped this week.
   - **Slipped** — didn't happen, and one honest word on why.
   - **Next** — what rolls into Monday.
2. Short. Celebrate the real win, name the real miss, no spin.

## Quarterly — OKR mode
- 1 objective (occasionally 2), each with 2–4 measurable key results.
- Mid/end-quarter: grade each KR 0.0–1.0, keep/kill/carry, and say what the next quarter's focus is.

## Concrete example (Monday output)
> **This week's ONE thing:** Ship self-serve onboarding — it's the lever on activation and the July investor ask.
> **Also:** (1) Close Acme contract. (2) Hire screen — 3 candidates. (3) Fix the billing bug.
> **Not this week:** Rebrand, conference talk, new pricing page. Parked on purpose.

## Quality bar (every time)
- **Exactly one** top thing on Monday, justified. Not three co-equal "top" items.
- "Also" list capped (≤5) and ranked; a "not this week" list exists.
- Friday recap is honest about slips, with a one-line why.
- OKRs are measurable — a stranger could tell if you hit them.

## When NOT to use
- A single upcoming meeting's prep/notes → /meeting-kit.
- The investor-facing monthly write-up → /investor-update.
- Turning a big goal into a build spec → /spec and Chief of Staff.
- Deep strategy (ICP, positioning) → GTM Strategist / Product Strategist.
