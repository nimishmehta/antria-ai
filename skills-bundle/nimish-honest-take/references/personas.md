# Persona casting

Two experts. Both seasoned (15–30+ years), both skeptical, both have seen products like this fail. They are not the same person wearing two hats — they want different things and judge by different yardsticks. Cast them specifically for the target's industry; bring the real knowledge that person would have.

## The buyer-side persona (the ICP)
The person who would actually pay for and live with this. They judge by: *does this kill a pain I feel, at a price that beats my alternative?*

Give them:
- **Title & context** — the real role that holds the budget or feels the pain (e.g., a CFO, a Head of People, a staff engineer, a clinic owner, a procurement lead).
- **Years & scars** — what they've bought before, what burned them, what they've learned to distrust.
- **Their alternatives** — the status quo, the incumbents, the "do nothing," the spreadsheet. A buyer always compares to *something*.
- **How they rank pain** — by severity and by dollars, not by feature coolness.
- **Their deal-breakers** — switching cost, trust, integration, compliance, lock-in.

## The seller-side persona (the operator)
A veteran who has built, sold, or run a business in this exact space. They judge by: *does this win deals and keep them, and is it defensible?*

Give them:
- **Title & context** — founder/operator/veteran practitioner who knows the GTM motion (e.g., a 30-year broker, a serial fintech founder, a former platform PM, a channel-sales veteran).
- **Market knowledge** — the real competitors by name, their wedges, pricing, and where they're weak.
- **Economics** — how money is actually made here (commission, SaaS, take-rate, services), CAC/retention realities, what trades at a premium.
- **Build-vs-buy instinct** — what's worth building vs. bundling vs. integrating.
- **What they've watched fail** — the graveyard of products that solved the wrong half of the problem.

## Worked examples (adapt; don't copy)
- **Employee-benefits / healthtech:** buyer = a CFO or Head of People at a 100–1,000-employee firm who's been burned by renewal hikes; seller = a 30-year benefits broker who has run both mid-market and enterprise books and knows Garner / Included Health / Mineral / Employee Navigator cold.
- **Dev tooling:** buyer = a staff engineer / platform lead who owns the build pipeline and hates yak-shaving; seller = a former DevTools founder who knows the open-source-to-paid conversion math and the incumbents.
- **Vertical SaaS:** buyer = the operator who runs the daily workflow (clinic manager, shop owner); seller = a vertical-SaaS operator who knows the switching costs and the system-of-record incumbent.
- **Fintech / payments:** buyer = a finance lead who fears reconciliation and compliance risk; seller = a payments veteran who knows interchange economics and the regulatory traps.

## Rules
- Name names. Real competitors and alternatives, or the analysis is generic and worthless.
- Both personas are allowed — required — to call BS. They should be the kind of expert who'd embarrass you in a meeting if you hand-waved.
- Keep each persona's voice consistent inside their own document.
