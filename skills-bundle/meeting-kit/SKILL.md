# Meeting Kit

Two jobs: get the founder walking in prepared, and walk them out with decisions and owned actions. Never dump a transcript.

## Before the meeting — pre-brief + agenda
1. Find the meeting on the calendar (Google Calendar / Outlook connector) — who's coming, when, how long.
2. Pre-brief (half a page max): who each attendee is, what happened last time with them, the one outcome this meeting needs, any landmines.
3. Agenda — **timeboxed**. Every item gets minutes that add up to the meeting length. Lead with the decision that must be made.
4. Hand the founder both, plus the single "if we only get one thing done, it's this."

Example agenda (30 min):
- 0–5 Context + goal for today
- 5–20 Decision: pricing tier for enterprise (need: yes/no on $X)
- 20–27 Open risks
- 27–30 Actions + owners

## After the meeting — minutes + actions
1. Pull the source: Zoom recording/notes if connected, otherwise the founder's notes.
2. Write minutes as **decisions and actions, not a transcript.** Three blocks:
   - **Decisions made** — one line each.
   - **Action items** — each has an owner (a name) and a due date. No ownerless actions.
   - **Open questions** — parked, with who follows up.
3. Keep it to a page. If someone reads only the actions block, they know what to do.

## Quality bar (every time)
- Agenda minutes sum to the real meeting length; decision-first ordering.
- Minutes = decisions + actions, never a play-by-play.
- **Every action has a named owner and a due date.** No orphans.
- Pre-brief fits on half a page and names the one required outcome.

## When NOT to use
- Writing the invite email or a follow-up note → /email-drafts.
- A recurring weekly planning/recap ritual → /weekly-review.
- Turning meeting outcomes into a formal document → /docx.
- Scheduling logistics only (no prep needed) → just use the calendar connector directly.
