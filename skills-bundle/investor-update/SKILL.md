# Investor Update

The monthly note that keeps investors informed and useful. Investors fund founders who report honestly and ask clearly. Follow internal-comms clarity: plain, tight, no spin.

## How to build
1. **Metrics block first** — at the top, scannable:
   - MRR (and month-over-month change), burn, runway (months of cash left), cash in bank.
   - Pipeline / new customers / key funnel number for this business.
   - Show the delta vs last month, not just the level.
2. **Narrative — three parts, short:**
   - **Wins** — 2–4 real ones.
   - **Lowlights** — what went wrong or slipped. Be honest; this is where trust is built. Never hide a miss.
   - **Asks** — specific and actionable: intros to named-type people, hiring help, a decision you want a reaction to. "Any thoughts welcome" is not an ask.
3. **Render** as an editable Word doc (/docx) or a PDF (/make-pdf) depending on how the founder sends it. Default to PDF for a clean send, docx if they want to tweak first.

## Concrete example (top of the update)
> **June 2026 — [Company]**
> MRR $42k (+9% MoM) · Burn $58k · Runway 11 mo · Cash $640k
> New logos: 6 · Pipeline: $180k
>
> **Wins:** Closed Acme (largest deal to date). Shipped self-serve onboarding.
> **Lowlights:** Churned two SMBs; onboarding still too manual — it's the July focus.
> **Asks:** Warm intros to Series-A B2B fintech GTM leaders. Feedback on our enterprise pricing (attached).

## Quality bar (every time)
- **Numbers at the very top**, with deltas. Runway stated in months.
- **Honest lowlights** — at least the real one. No update is all wins.
- **Asks are specific** — an investor could act on them today.
- Fits on one to two pages. Skimmable in 90 seconds.

## When NOT to use
- Building the underlying financial model / cap table math → /xlsx and Finance Lead.
- A fundraising pitch deck → /pptx.
- Internal team update → /weekly-review.
- Board deck for a formal board meeting → /pptx (board-update mode).
