# Support

## Getting help

**Something is broken** → [open a bug report](https://github.com/nimishmehta/antria-ai/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml)

**Something is missing** → [request a feature](https://github.com/nimishmehta/antria-ai/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml)

**How does X work?** → [antria.ai](https://www.antria.ai) covers how it works, the full team, and what it can do.

**Something sensitive or security-related** → see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not use a public issue.

## Before you report a problem

A few things resolve most issues:

1. **Check you're on the latest version.** Antria updates itself, but a long-closed app may be behind. Reopen it and let it update.
2. **Check your AI plan is connected and has capacity.** Antria runs on your own Claude or Codex plan. If you have hit a usage limit, tasks will stop partway.
3. **Check the folder.** Antria works inside the folder you pointed it at. If results are landing somewhere unexpected, that is usually the first place to look.

## What to include

The more of this you give, the faster it gets fixed:

- Your operating system and version
- The Antria version (shown in the app)
- Which AI engine you are running on — Claude or Codex
- What you asked the team to do, in your own words
- What happened instead

Redact anything confidential first. Issues are public.

## Response times

This is a free tool maintained by one person alongside other work. You can
expect a reply within a week. Security reports are prioritised.
