## Founders Office v0.1.2 — caveman notes

_Fixes only. No new feature. Just calmer, cleaner._

**FOUNDER CHANNEL — big cleanup:**
- 🏠 Your home channel no more a tangled scroll. **Chat is the main column now, stuck to the typing box** — ask something, it lands right at the bottom where you type. No more question jumping UP above the updates.
- 📥 The team's auto-updates moved OUT of the chat into a **slim rail on the side** — tap to open (Right Now / Today / Earlier), a little "N new" badge when stuff happened. Your talk and your office's activity finally separate.
- Only BIG moments (team started / shipped / got stuck) whisper one quiet line into chat. "Needs you" stays pinned at top.

**DELIVERABLES — stop the pile-up:**
- 📄 A task's Done card now shows **only the files THAT task made** — not old files from earlier in the channel, not other channels' files, not app internals. A plain question shows no files at all.

**INPUT BOX:**
- ✍️ Removed the dead Image button and the cartoon emoji — clean line-icons, grown-up look.

_All the earlier 0.1.1 fixes ride along too (try-again no longer eats work, Stop really stops, first-run authorize, usage message on old Claude Code, design connector, Vercel auto-install)._
